01 — How it works

Your edits are the training data.

No fine-tuning and no prompt guesswork. The model stays fixed. What evolves is a readable style adapter wrapped around it.

adapter
v0
i.

Seed it

A few instructions or a style guide. Claude writes the first draft.

ii.

Edit as usual

Every change you make is a labeled example of your taste.

iii.

Rules emerge

Repeated edits become plain-language rules, each backed by the edits that support it.

iv.

Only proven changes ship

A new adapter must beat the current one on drafts you already corrected, or it is discarded. Then the loop starts again.

02 — The gate

Scored by you, not by another model.

Asking an AI to grade "good writing" just swaps your taste for its taste. StyleLoop holds back drafts you have already corrected, has the candidate adapter re-edit them, and measures how close the result lands to your real edits.

// adopted only if gain beats the current version gain = sim(adapter's edit, your edit) − sim(draft, your edit) // rule backed by 1 edit → observation (not used)
