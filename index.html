StyleLoop

Dial in your taste.

A writing engine that learns your style from the edits you make, and keeps a change only when it measurably brings drafts closer to how you would have written them.

How it works

  1. Seed it. Give it a few instructions or a style guide. Claude writes a first draft.
  2. Edit as usual. Every edit you make becomes a labeled example of your taste.
  3. It proposes rules. StyleLoop turns repeated edits into a readable style adapter: plain rules, each backed by the edits that support it.
  4. Only proven changes ship. Each new adapter re-edits held-out drafts you already corrected. It is adopted only if its output gets closer to your actual edits than the current version does.

The model stays fixed. What evolves is the harness around it: the adapter, its evidence, and the gate that decides what is kept. Your corrections are the score, not another model's opinion of "good writing".
